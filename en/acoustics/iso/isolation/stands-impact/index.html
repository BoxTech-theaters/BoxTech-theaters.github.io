Jumping in the bowl reaches the suite through the frame. Glass does not stop it.

A structural break, or a resilient base under a sensitive room, is studied with the engineer before the bowl is poured, not after the complaint.

A rigid path

Airborne insulation of the glass stops part of the crowd in the air. Vibration takes the steel and the concrete into the suite floor and the commentator’s desk. A microphone on the desk picks it up even when the glass is good. The remedy is a break in the frame, or a mass on isolators under the room, or at least a desk top that is not rigidly tied to the slab. This is not a ceiling-absorber line. It is written with the structural engineer at the start, because changing it after installation costs more than the wall.
