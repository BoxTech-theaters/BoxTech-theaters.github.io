Insulation atlas/Detail · floating floor

A floating floor is mass on a resilient layer. One rigid point is enough to spoil it.

A hall over a classroom, a dance floor over rooms, or a bowl over lockers. The line is L'nT,w after the finishes, not before the carpet.

The sequence

The structural slab, then a continuous resilient layer with no rigid gaps, then the floating mass, then the finish. A resilient strip at every wall so the finish does not touch the wall. The skirting is not fixed to both floor and wall. A pipe or a light base that pierces the resilient layer is a bridge. It is wrapped or separated. Measure after the final floor, because a later layer can short the gap. A carpet alone lowers the high-frequency click of a heel and does not treat a low rhythm.

  • Do not store boards on the resilient layer and then remove them leaving a crushed point unchecked.
  • A resilient ceiling under the slab is the alternative when the floor above cannot be touched.
